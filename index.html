Common Questions

Everything you
need to know

What does an insurance broker do that I can't do myself?
+
A broker compares dozens of insurers simultaneously, negotiates on your behalf, and ensures the policy actually fits your situation — not just the cheapest option that could leave gaps.
Do I pay extra to use a broker?
+
No. Brokers are paid by insurance companies through commissions. You get expert advice and broader market access at no extra cost to you.
How quickly can I get a Super Visa insurance quote?
+
Often the same day. Priya can usually have options ready within hours. Coverage certificates are issued quickly for visa applications.
I'm a newcomer to Canada. Where do I start?
+
Start with a free consultation. Priya specializes in helping newcomers understand Canadian insurance in English or Hindi.
What's the difference between bank mortgage insurance and independent?
+
Bank creditor insurance declines in value as your mortgage shrinks. Independent coverage is portable, personally owned, and often provides better value.
Which areas of Ontario do you serve?
+
All of Ontario — with a strong focus on Brampton, Mississauga, Toronto, Vaughan, and Oakville. Virtual consultations available province-wide.
