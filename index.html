Common Questions

Everything you
need to know

What does an insurance broker do that I can't do myself?
+
A broker compares dozens of insurers simultaneously, negotiates on your behalf, and ensures the policy fits your situation. Priya explains every option in plain language — in English or Hindi — so you decide with confidence.
Do I pay extra to use a broker?
+
No. Brokers are paid by insurance companies through commissions. You get expert, unbiased advice and access to 20+ insurers at absolutely no extra cost to you. Priya’s consultations are always free.
How quickly can I get a Super Visa insurance quote?
+
Often the same day — sometimes within hours. Coverage certificates are issued quickly for IRCC visa applications. Priya regularly assists families across Brampton and Mississauga where Super Visa demand is highest.
I'm a newcomer to Canada. Where do I start?
+
Start with a free consultation. Priya came to Canada as an immigrant herself 20 years ago — she understands exactly what you’re going through. She explains everything in English or Hindi, covering health, life, Super Visa, and investment options.
What's the difference between bank mortgage insurance and independent?
+
Bank creditor insurance is tied to your mortgage, declines in value as the balance drops, and ends when you switch lenders. Independent coverage is personally owned, portable, stays the same value, and is typically 30–40% cheaper.
Which areas of Ontario do you serve?
+
All of Ontario and Alberta. Priya has a strong focus on the GTA — Brampton, Mississauga, Toronto, Vaughan, Oakville — and offers virtual consultations for anyone across Canada.
